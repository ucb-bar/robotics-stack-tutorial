# Walkthroughs

- `MB_MAXPOOL_WALKTHROUGH.md` covers the lab's kernel: how a 2×2 max pool uses MBP.MAX8, the three
  pieces of evidence that it runs on the accelerator, why spike and the board disagree, and exercises.
- `MB_GELU_WALKTHROUGH.md` is the contrast. The kernel gets 43 to 49× faster with no accelerator, from a
  change of algorithm instead, and the walkthrough ends with an exercise in finding a bug.

To read one formatted, open its context menu and choose Open With → Markdown Preview.
